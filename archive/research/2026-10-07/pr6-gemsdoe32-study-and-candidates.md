# Superseded GEMSDOE32 draft — correction and withdrawal

This note is retained only to record that an earlier draft made an unsupported attribution. Its prior score table, causal narrative, and candidate-ranking claims have been removed: they were not sufficient evidence, and reproducing standings is not permitted under the project's reading of the DrivenData Terms of Use.

## Corrected result status

The earlier draft linked a public `0.2778` value to GEMSDOE32 `H33-2-B2` and described it as a scored result. That link is **unsupported and contradicted**, not fact. The sibling project's owner-published page labels H33-2-B2 **UNSCORED** and calls `0.2747` a modelled projection; it says no organizer score exists for that artifact. No organizer-authenticated mapping from the exact scored TIFF to a score has been verified. Do not use the withdrawn draft's explanation as a causal account of any competition result.

The official distance-weighted Tversky metric can motivate a test of sparsification, but the metric alone does not identify why a submission scored. Hidden fault labels, weighted TP/FP, and the exact scored artifact are needed for causal attribution.
