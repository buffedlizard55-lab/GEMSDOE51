# Archived outputs (not submission recommendations)

The files under `submissions/2026-10-06/` are prior outputs and receipts retained only for audit and uniqueness comparisons. They are **not eligible downloads**, were not newly created in the current review, and must not be submitted. In particular, some zero-outside GeoTIFFs fail the official null/NaN-outside format requirement. The current site intentionally does not serve them from `docs/downloads/`.

The source-aware builder scans archived TIFFs when checking support uniqueness; moving old rasters out of the public download directory does not exempt them from the gate. Current status and machine-readable promotion decision are in `../data/submission_manifest.json`.

`site-artifacts/2026-10-06/`, `evidence/legacy-2026-10-06/`, `data/legacy-2026-10-06/`, `research/2026-10-06/`, and `registry/2026-10-06/` contain superseded local receipts, holdouts, and pre-registration text from an older implementation. They are preserved as historical evidence, not current results or organizer scores. The archived research digest is explicitly marked superseded. Consult the current evidence under `../data/`, `../knowledge/`, and `../registry/` first.
