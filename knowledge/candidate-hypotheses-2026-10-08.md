## Source audit and locked feature definition before code

The source/page and local-band audit is recorded in [`evidence/h53_k2x_input_audit_20261008.json`](../evidence/h53_k2x_input_audit_20261008.json). The pinned training raster's band 17 is described only as `cond_surf - Conductivity surface - electrical conductivity of subsurface`; it has no unit, inversion/source, calibration, or depth metadata. The values are positive, but that does not establish conductivity units or physical interpretation. The organizer's page lists surface conductivity and depth to conductive base, while the login-walled training raster mirror is not organizer-authenticated. GDR's five MT conductance layers integrate conductivity across depth intervals from 2 to 200 km and are **not** evidence that they are the source of `cond_surf`; GeoDAWN documents airborne magnetic/radiometric surveys, not this conductivity band. Therefore the feature below uses globally empirical-rank-transformed layers and makes no unit, depth, salinity, alteration, or geothermal-temperature claim.

Before implementation, H53-K2x is locked to one feature `h53_k2x_min_s2s5`: use rank-prepared `comp_cond_surf`, `comp_depth_to_base_surf`, `comp_iso_grav_anom`, and `comp_tmi`, with the common finite/footprint mask. At each Gaussian derivative scale σ=2 and 5 px, normalize each structural gradient magnitude by its own deterministic row-major sampled q95 (at most about 200,000 common pixels), clip to [0,1], and form an unoriented gradient-normal tensor from depth, gravity, and TMI. Structural edge strength is mean normalized magnitude times tensor axial coherence. Project the conductivity gradient into the tensor normal and tangent; multiply structural edge strength by the clipped conductivity-gradient amplitude (relative to its sampled q95) and the positive fractional cross-edge-minus-along-edge energy, `max(g_normal² − g_tangent², 0)/(||g_cond||² + 1e-12)`. The final single feature is the pixelwise minimum of the two scale-specific values, NaN where either derivative support is invalid. If q95 is effectively zero, its normalized strength is zero. No labels are used in the feature; no threshold or scale may be selected after seeing the holdout.

Source checks were fetched on 2026-10-08: the official DrivenData problem page identifies faults as the target and lists surface conductivity/depth-to-conductive-base features; the GDR 1391 record confirms its public CC-BY INGENIOUS inventory and as-is caveat; the USGS ScienceBase record explains its distinct depth-integrated MT conductance product; USGS GeoDAWN describes magnetic/radiometric survey data. Hoffmann-Rothe et al. describe one northern Chile MT conductor correlated with damage/alteration, whereas Ritter et al. report materially different fault electrical structures (including no conductor at one active transform) and explicitly note non-uniqueness. The USGS Great Basin papers support multi-evidence/depth context, not this pixelwise operator. Source-by-source claims and limitations are in the audit receipt.
