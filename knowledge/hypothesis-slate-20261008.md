## H53-A locked experiment

- **Stage 1 (separate, broad tiles only):** calculate clipped-trace horizontal tensors on 10 km cells using the source-documented Kostrov/Kreemer summation, then calculate predicted dilation and source-defined shear. Compute raw residuals `r_dil = observed_dilation - predicted_dilation / 1e-8` and `r_shear = observed_shear - predicted_shear / 1e-8`. The literal source notation is `10E-9/yr` (= `1e-8/yr`); the competing `1e-9/yr` reading is retained as a sensitivity, not chosen after the Stage-2 score. The nominal source slip component is treated as fault-plane slip; vertical slip, 60° normal dip and omitted unknown-sense traces are explicit assumptions. The approved coarse mask is the top 90% of the preregistered equal-weight mean of the empirical ranks of `r_dil` and `r_shear`, not a fine-scale location. For each spatial fold, remove the entire official trace record if any of its segments intersects the held-out block before computing its Stage-1 mask.
- **Stage 1 score (reported separately):** five deterministic whole-record splits; for each, report q10 approved area, held-out visible-catalogue pixel recall, lift (recall/area), and uniform-in-mask versus uniform-in-footprint DTI. This is known-catalogue proxy evidence, not vent discovery.
- **Stage 2:** train the existing H-H/H-D fixed 50/50 probability blend and the H53-A extension on the same six contiguous 3×3 spatial blocks, 12-pixel training buffer, 250,000 background negatives per fold and frozen seeds. Add only the locked H53-A edge-pair features to H-H; use H-D as the paired unchanged arm. Use STE L9, continuity `0.35`, point spacing `2.4 px`, `M/|G|=3.47`, 2 px published-catalogue exclusion, and a fold-specific Stage-1 q10 allowed mask. Emit only inside approved tiles.
- **Feature definition:** at Gaussian scales σ=2 and 5 pixels, compute mask-normalized gradients of detrended elevation and isostatic gravity. Rank-normalize nonzero gradient magnitudes over the footprint. For each surface-edge normal, compare zero-lag edge-normal agreement with the maximum gravity-edge agreement sampled 1–8 pixels along either side of that normal. Freeze six inputs: zero-lag co-edge response, maximum offset co-edge response, and winning offset divided by 8 at each scale. Invalid samples remain missing; no label or fold truth enters feature construction.
- **Promotion rule:** require the fresh ungated H-H/H-D comparator to reproduce frozen mean proxy DTI `0.285340602656319` within `1e-5`; require candidate q10-gated Stage-2 mean to exceed both that frozen mean and its same-fold gated comparator, positive in at least 4/6 paired folds, q10 approved area ≥2/3, Stage-1 lift ≤1.5, and every emitted point inside the approved mask. Do not tune or rerun H53-A after seeing its result. No competition slot is authorized by a local proxy score alone.
- **Artifact policy:** if the candidate fails promotion, a newly computed TIFF may be retained only as an explicitly research-only diagnostic (`DOWNLOAD FOR RESEARCH: YES; SUBMIT: NO`). If it passes the local gates, that permits a clearly named upload candidate, not a claim of portal acceptance or a leaderboard score. The output must be one-band float32, exact sample-submission CRS/shape/transform, finite values in `[0,1]`, zero outside the footprint, and pass the scoped uniqueness audit. Portal acceptance remains untested absent an authenticated DrivenData submission.

## Source/target limits

The contest target is fault pixels, not geothermal vents. The local labels are the visible USGS/INGENIOUS catalogue (and are byte-identical to `existing_faults.tif`); holdouts therefore test recovery of already mapped fault patterns, not genuinely unknown expert labels. Officially documented joint geologic/geodetic deformation modeling motivates the *budget comparison*, but does not prove that a residual is a missing fault: off-fault deformation, aseismic deformation, rate error, dip/rake assumptions, geodetic convention and interpolation remain alternatives. The sources and formula audit are linked in `knowledge/official-source-review-20261007.md`.
