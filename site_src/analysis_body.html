GEMSDOE32 result review — attribution kept within the evidence

The GEMSDOE32 owner page presents H33-2-B2 as a projected 0.2747 artifact with 37,654 dots and a 200 m prune around the known-fault catalogue. The same page marks it UNSCORED and states that no organizer score exists for that artifact. The page's reported +0.00487 over a 0.2708 base is an owner-reported live-mirror/model comparison, not a DrivenData score linked to those file bytes.

What can be hypothesized: under the official distance-weighted Tversky metric, emitted mass has a false-positive cost. Removing dots near mapped faults could improve credit per emitted unit if those dots are less likely to receive hidden-fault credit. The official problem also defines the initial hidden test set as expert-identified faults not present in the existing public database. That supplies a plausible mechanism for testing a catalogue-flank prune, but it does not show that this mechanism caused H33-2-B2's projected gain or any official score.

What cannot be claimed: the artifact has no organizer-returned score, no independently reproduced test result, and no controlled official comparison linked to the exact file. Do not call 0.2747 a leaderboard score, do not attribute a causal gain to the 200 m prune, and do not assume the reported result transfers to GEMSDOE51. The artifact is owner-published evidence of a design, not proof of performance.

GEMSDOE51 evidence, stages reported separately

Stage 1 uses the Kreemer et al. (2000) Eq. 3 fault-slip tensor only as a coarse tile prior. In the corrected trace-held-out test, the deficit has Spearman 0.03916 and top-50% lift 1.08290; geodetic-only scores 0.09930 and 1.23864. Stage 1 is weak. A hard q50 gate reduces mean Stage-2 proxy DTI from 0.28164 to 0.18153. Formula, assumptions, and full split evidence are on the method page and in the source-linked research record.

Stage 2 H-D baseline is 0.2816627 mean proxy DTI and 0.6726183 mean AUC across six blocked folds at M/|G|=3.47. The narrowed H51-X1 implementation (rank-normalized total magnetic and isostatic-gravity bands only; planned RTP/HG/VG derivative bands were not used by the new transform, though they remained in the common baseline stack) returned 0.2796713 mean proxy DTI, delta -0.0019913, and wins 3/6 folds; it failed promotion. This partial test does not falsify every version of the broader preregistered layer-family hypothesis. A soft w=0.20 Stage-1 adjustment narrowly cleared the numeric holdout gate, but the in-memory map was too similar to an existing candidate (Jaccard 0.86130), so the build stopped before a TIFF was written.

Bottom line

The defensible lesson from the GEMSDOE32 page is to test sparse, credit-efficient emissions and explain their mechanism—not to assign an unscored projection a cause.
