The official metric is the distance-weighted Tversky index: DTI = TP_w / (0.2*(TP_w + FP_w) + 0.8*|G|), with a triangular 300 m distance-credit kernel. That equation explains how an authenticated prediction would be scored; it does not reveal which raster or geology produced an unauthenticated participant row. Without the exact scored TIFF, hidden fault geometry, and weighted TP/FP terms, the geological cause of a public score cannot be identified.

What can be hypothesized: under the official metric, emitted mass has false-positive cost. Removing dots near mapped faults could improve credit per emitted unit if those dots are less likely to receive hidden-fault credit. The official problem defines the initial hidden test set as expert-identified faults not present in the existing public database. This motivates a falsifiable catalogue-flank-pruning experiment, but it does not show that such a mechanism caused H33-2-B2's projection or any organizer result.

What cannot be claimed: H33-2-B2 has no organizer-returned score, no independently reproduced hidden-label result, and no controlled official comparison linked to the exact file. Do not call 0.2747 a leaderboard result, do not attribute 0.2778 to H33-2-B2, and do not claim a causal gain from the reported prune. The artifact documents a design idea, not performance proof.

GEMSDOE51 evidence — Stage 1 and Stage 2 reported separately

Stage 1: the Kreemer et al. (2000) Eq. 3 fault-slip tensor is intended only as a coarse tile prior. The historical five-split trace receipt recorded deficit Spearman 0.03916 and top-half lift 1.08290, below geodetic-only 0.09930 and 1.23864; it predates the current signed-shear and selectable-rate implementation. A separate four-scenario formula audit did not exclude held-out trace rows from the catalogue-derived budget and is not a valid holdout. No current-formula, leakage-controlled Stage-1 rerun is available because raw rasters and prepared arrays are absent. The prior remains a research question, not a validated fine-scale locator or hard gate. See the method and formula page and Stage-1 reconciliation.

Stage 2: H-D has mean proxy DTI 0.2816627 and mean AUC 0.6726183 across six blocked folds at M/|G|=3.47. The narrowed H51-X1 implementation (rank-normalized total magnetic and isostatic-gravity bands only; planned RTP/HG/VG derivatives were not inputs to the new transform) returned 0.2796713 mean proxy DTI, delta -0.0019913, and won 3/6 folds; it failed promotion. This partial test does not falsify every version of the broader preregistered family.

The H-G+H-D experiment has a research-only NaN-outside GeoTIFF linked on the executive summary. The in-footprint predictions are a format re-encoding, not a new model run. The matched proxy DTI is 0.2810564 vs H-D 0.2816627 (paired delta -0.0006063; 3/6 folds higher). It passes a scoped uniqueness audit and a historical q70 non-dominance diagnostic on the archived Stage-1 field used to create it; the current-formula map was not rerun. It loses the incumbent holdout and is NOT FOR SUBMISSION. H-M and STE have higher mean points but inconclusive intervals, fail packaged-artifact uniqueness, and lack current q70 Stage-1 checks. No slot is authorized.

Bottom line

The defensible lesson from the GEMSDOE32 page is to test sparse, credit-efficient emissions and explain their mechanism—not to assign an unscored projection a cause. GEMSDOE51 has no upload-eligible file and makes no organizer-score claim. Local spatial holdouts are visible-catalogue proxies, not hidden-label validation.
