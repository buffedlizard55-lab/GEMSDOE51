Why the best prior submission scored what it did

The group's best owner claim is 0.2778 (gemsdoe32-h33-h33-2-b2). Two of that family's artifacts give us a free natural experiment, because one is the other with 6,436 dots deleted.

The measurement

Measured here: the deleted set is exactly the emitted pixels at Euclidean distance 1–2 px from a catalogued fault pixel; the retained set all lie at ≥ 2.236 px. Writing K = 0.8·|G|:

TP(44 090) = 0.2600 · (0.2·44 090 + K) = 2 292.68 + 0.2600 K
TP(37 654) = 0.2778 · (0.2·37 654 + K) = 2 092.06 + 0.2778 K
ΔTP        =   200.62 − 0.0178 K

ΔTP ≥ 0 bounds the hidden truth at |G| ≤ 14 090 px. With the group's own generative truth model (12 691 px) → ΔTP ≈ 20. So the 6,436 deleted dots earned 0.003 credit each against 0.139 for the dots that were kept — a factor of 45. Predicting within 200 m of the catalogue is worth essentially nothing. That is consistent with the organizer's confirmation that known fault pixels are masked out of scoring entirely.

The kernel is k = 1 − d/3. A credit of 0.131 means an emitted dot sits on average 2.6 px = 260 m from the nearest hidden fault pixel; the leader's ~0.163 means 200 m. That is the entire game: it is a localisation problem, not a coverage problem. An isolated dot landing exactly on a straight trace earns about 3.0 credit, so a mean of 0.131 means only about 4 % of the group's dots land on a hidden trace.

Why the dotted design worked, and where it runs out

Thinning a thick probability surface to isolated dots at ~2.4 px spacing removes mass that was already covered by a neighbouring dot, which is exactly the objective the metric rewards: 0.1922 (continuous field) → 0.2477 → 0.2600 → 0.2778 as the emission got sparser and better spaced. It runs out when the dots start landing off the traces. Our holdout in-block AUC is 0.673 and our proxy DTI at matched mass is 0.282, so our detector is about as good as the one that produced 0.2778. Beating 0.3774 needs a materially better field, not a better emission rule.

Honest expectation for this submission

Our instrument says 0.282 ± 0.02 at the live mass ratio, which brackets the group's best and sits well below the current leader of 0.3774. Expect this file to land in the high 0.2s. That expectation is written down before the score is known.
