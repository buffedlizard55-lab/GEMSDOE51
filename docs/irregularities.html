IR-51-14The blocked-holdout instrument is structurally blind to catalogue-suppressed positional refinement. The snap surface that H-51-L moves dots along MUST be suppressed around the mapped catalogue (mass within ~200 m of mapped faults is dead mass live - the mechanism behind the group's 0.2600 -> 0.2708 -> 0.2778 gains by B-pruning). But in the blocked holdout the pretend-hidden truth IS the block's catalogue pixels, so suppression zeroes the surface exactly where the instrument's truth sits. A refinement that works live can therefore never be rewarded by this holdout, and one that passes this holdout would be suspect live. H-51-L was falsified on the instrument anyway (window=2 -0.0033 DTI at conserved mass; window=1 neutral), but the instrument's verdict on suppressed-surface refinements is bounded by this argument.scripts/run_refine_holdout.py + data/refine_holdout.json + evidence/refine_*.json (session 2026-10-07 sweep); the B-prune mechanism is documented in knowledge/02 section 1 from GEMSDOE32 Round 4.open - a refinement experiment needs either a non-catalogue truth model or a live slot with a pre-registered safety factor
