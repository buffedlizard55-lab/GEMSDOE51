What this is

GEMSDOE51 studies fault indications in the GeoDAWN region. Stage 1 is a coarse 10 km geodetic strain-budget diagnostic; Stage 2 is a fine-scale detector. The separately evaluated q10 Stage-1 deficit mask approved about 90% of the footprint, with weak trace-holdout enrichment. It was used as a fold-specific allowed domain, with zero Stage-1 score weight, for the earlier K1 test and the latest H53-K2x test; every tested Stage-2 point was inside its approved mask. The historical H-H/H-D benchmark predates this q10 condition and only 86.71% of its points fall inside the current full-map q10 mask, so it does not satisfy the all-points-inside constraint. H51-K1 and H53-K2x were not promoted, and all local holdout values remain visible-catalogue proxies, not organizer scores.

The three facts that decide everything

  1. Known faults are masked. DrivenData staff confirmed on 2026-09-16 that pixels corresponding to known USGS/INGENIOUS faults are excluded from evaluation, in both prize rounds. source
  2. "New fault" includes new geometry of an existing system. Continuations, splays and parallel strands count. source
  3. The metric is a budget. From the published definitions, DTI = TP_w / (0.2·(TP_w+FP_w) + 0.8·|G|). The hidden truth size |G| is fixed for an organizer evaluation but undisclosed locally, so a submission stands or falls on credit earned per unit of emitted mass. The break-even bar for a marginal pixel is c = 0.2·DTI.
