H51-1 — Stage-A geodetic strain-budget gate: top-half tiles by geodetic strain magnitude, after the brief's literal deficit rule was tested and refuted

rank 1 · status: REFUTED as a locator, then replaced. (a) Blocked holdout: the deficit's approved tiles concentrate held-out catalogue labels with lift 0.949, only 1/4 folds above 1 (rho -0.338). (b) Independent instrument 1 (lidar scarp intensity, in-fill H19-4 layer): rho -0.048. (c) Independent instrument 2 (off-catalogue SGMC fault density): rho -0.228. The adopted substitute, geodetic strain magnitude, is +0.376 (top-quartile lift 1.393) on the lidar instrument and -0.166 on the SGMC instrument: a split decision, reported as a split (IR-51-INST-01).

Layers

  • training_features.tif band 4 = geod_2ndinv (second invariant of the geodetic strain-rate tensor)
  • training_features.tif band 7 = geod_shearrate, band 8 = geod_dilaterate (comparison rules)
  • external/gdr_qfaults_traces.csv (INGENIOUS/GDR-1391 slip rates + clipped trace lengths)
  • external Siler (2022) slip-tendency table (per-sense dips, USGS ScienceBase 6296974d)

Signature targeted

Tile-level mass balance between what geodesy requires and what mapped faults can supply. 116 tiles of 25 km. Median geodetic second invariant 21.03 ns/yr; median mapped-fault equivalent 6.00 ns/yr; median ratio 0.455; median deficit 0.545.

Formula / mechanism

eps_geo = (1/(2*A_tile)) * sum_k L_k*u_k/sin(dip_k) -- Kreemer et al. (2000) EPSG 52:765-770 eq. (3), the fault-slip variant of the Kostrov (1974) summation; dip by slip sense from Siler (2022): normal 61.9 deg, right-lateral 85.8, left-lateral 81.6, unspecified 64.3 (medians of 83,234 measured rows).

Why it catches a fault missing from the catalogue

A tile whose geodetic strain is not matched by mapped-fault moment is, by construction, a place where structure exists that the catalogue does not contain. The gate is deliberately coarse (58 of 116 tiles, 2,537,841 px = 49% of the footprint) so that Stage B, not Stage A, decides placement.

How it differs from everything already implemented

No sibling repository used fault slip rates at all. GEMSDOE26 tested the geodetic deformation field as a *feature* (dilcond-oof, 0.1223); here the geodetic field is a physically derived budget whose residual is measured and, when found wanting, replaced by the validated statistic rather than smuggled into the emission.

Validation

REFUTED as a locator, then replaced. (a) Blocked holdout: the deficit's approved tiles concentrate held-out catalogue labels with lift 0.949, only 1/4 folds above 1 (rho -0.338). (b) Independent instrument 1 (lidar scarp intensity, in-fill H19-4 layer): rho -0.048. (c) Independent instrument 2 (off-catalogue SGMC fault density): rho -0.228. The adopted substitute, geodetic strain magnitude, is +0.376 (top-quartile lift 1.393) on the lidar instrument and -0.166 on the SGMC instrument: a split decision, reported as a split (IR-51-INST-01).

Expected gain vs cost

Cost: 2 h of table work + a 116-tile instrument. Expected gain as a locator: 0 (refuted); gain as a *mask* that removes 51% of the emission domain at no measured cost in credit: kept. This is the single most important negative result in this repository.

H51-2 — Lidar scarp-feature ensemble as the decisive fine-scale channel inside approved tiles (Stage B detector)

rank 2 · status: PROMOTED. Fold AUCs 0.6719 / 0.7987 / 0.7034 / 0.7331 (mean 0.7268) on the visible catalogue. Every alternative emitter trialled (credit-density ordering, redundancy-aware budget packing) FAILED the pre-registered promotion rule (mean paired contrast <= 0 or <3/4 folds) and is recorded as a negative result.

Layers

  • training_features.tif 19 official bands incl. band 6 tc (magnetic tilt/curvature), det_elev, tmi_hg, tmi_vg, rtp, iso_grav_anom_hg, cond_surf, deq_n100a15
  • external/geodawn_rad_u8.tif (K, Th, U, TC) and geodawn_extensions_u8.tif (ThK, UK, UTh, TMI_up150)
  • external/derived_sgmc_faults_100m_u8.tif used as a DISTANCE channel only

Signature targeted

HistGradientBoosting (220 iters, lr 0.08, 31 leaves) on 44 channels; 4 spatially blocked folds with a 600 m boundary band removed from training and scoring; belief field emitted as sparse 1-px dots at 3 px minimum separation, sized at 4% of the candidate area.

Formula / mechanism

—

Why it catches a fault missing from the catalogue

The hidden expert set was produced inside a geothermal program: it contains Quaternary-active structures whose surface expression is a scarp in lidar but which the state/USGS compilations, digitised before the 3DEP lidar coverage existed, do not carry. A scarp-sharpness ensemble detects the *physical* fault trace, not its administrative record.

How it differs from everything already implemented

The sibling repositories used the same scarp layer mostly as one arm among many and never validated a belief FIELD against a published sibling field on identical masks. This repository does: AUC 0.7268 vs 0.6674 (oof_B) and 0.6687 (oof_D), higher on 4/4 folds; paired matched-mass DTI +0.0482 (8/8).

Validation

PROMOTED. Fold AUCs 0.6719 / 0.7987 / 0.7034 / 0.7331 (mean 0.7268) on the visible catalogue. Every alternative emitter trialled (credit-density ordering, redundancy-aware budget packing) FAILED the pre-registered promotion rule (mean paired contrast <= 0 or <3/4 folds) and is recorded as a negative result.

Expected gain vs cost

Cost: ~1 day (feature stack 2.16 GB, fold models). Measured gain: +0.0482 mean paired DTI at matched mass versus the best sibling out-of-fold field, on the only controlled instrument available. Transfers to the leaderboard only if the hidden set behaves like the holdout blocks.

H51-3 — Literal strain-budget DEFICIT (geodetic minus mapped-fault moment) as the Stage-A prior: implemented, tested, FALSIFIED

rank 3 · status: FALSIFIED on all three available instruments (blocked holdout label density lift 0.949, 1/4 folds; rho -0.048 on lidar scarp intensity; rho -0.228 on off-catalogue SGMC density). Retained in the pipeline as a diagnostic column and in the site as the headline negative result.

Layers

  • geod_2ndinv, geod_shearrate, geod_dilaterate (official bands 4/7/8)
  • INGENIOUS/GDR-1391 slip rates x clipped lengths
  • Siler (2022) per-sense dips

Signature targeted

Residual = geodetic second invariant minus the mapped-fault equivalent computed with the published formula. Under the brief it should mark the missing moment. It does not.

Formula / mechanism

—

Why it catches a fault missing from the catalogue

The physical argument is sound in principle (Zeng & Shen 2016 put a large off-fault moment rate in California; 17% of the worldwide GSRM moment rate sits in diffuse zones), which is exactly why the test was run. A sound argument with a negative test result must be reported as a negative result.

How it differs from everything already implemented

No sibling repository tested this rule at all; it comes from the task brief and from the literature cited above. It is reported here in full, including the formula, the numbers, and the two independent instruments that refute it.

Validation

FALSIFIED on all three available instruments (blocked holdout label density lift 0.949, 1/4 folds; rho -0.048 on lidar scarp intensity; rho -0.228 on off-catalogue SGMC density). Retained in the pipeline as a diagnostic column and in the site as the headline negative result.

Expected gain vs cost

Cost: 2 h. Measured gain: 0 as a locator. Value: it prevents a 0.3195-beating strategy from being built on an unvalidated premise.

H51-4 — Per-sense dip and slip-sense weighting inside the Kostrov sum (rigorous version of the Stage-A formula)

rank 4 · status: PARTIAL. Dips measured and applied (normal 61.9, RL 85.8, LL 81.6, unspecified 64.3 deg); the fraction of trace length with a resolved sense is 36% of total length (6,286 of 17,375 km inside the footprint). The full moment-tensor version is NOT TESTED: the joined table has strike and dip but no rake.

Layers

  • Siler (2022) slip- and dilation-tendency tables (83,234 rows with Dip and slip_sense in the footprint)
  • INGENIOUS trace table (868 normal, 87 right-lateral, 76 left-lateral, 95 other)

Signature targeted

Replace the single assumed 60-degree dip with the measured per-sense dip distribution, and (next) build the full unit-moment tensor m_ij from strike and dip rather than the scalar contraction. The tensor version requires a rake, which the joined table does not carry; the strike and dip columns do exist, so a partial tensor is available.

Formula / mechanism

—

Why it catches a fault missing from the catalogue

Not a detection idea: a measurement-quality idea. It changes the *magnitude* of the summed strain but not, to first order, the tile pattern (a scalar multiplier cancels in a quantile gate), so its value is scientific rather than score-bearing - unless the tensor version reveals that strike-slip and normal tiles need different geodetic bands.

How it differs from everything already implemented

The siblings used no slip rates and no dips at all; this is the only place in the family where the trace geometry and the per-sense dips are joined to the official strain-rate bands.

Validation

PARTIAL. Dips measured and applied (normal 61.9, RL 85.8, LL 81.6, unspecified 64.3 deg); the fraction of trace length with a resolved sense is 36% of total length (6,286 of 17,375 km inside the footprint). The full moment-tensor version is NOT TESTED: the joined table has strike and dip but no rake.

Expected gain vs cost

Cost for the partial version: done. Cost for the tensor version: ~3 h given a rake source (none is currently obtainable for free; INGENIOUS carries sense, not rake). Expected score gain: 0 unless the tensor reveals a band choice that the scalar hides. Ranked below the detection ideas for that reason.

H51-5 — Independent-compilation gap closure: score lineaments by agreement with the pre-Quaternary state map (SGMC) and Siler (2022) slip tendency

rank 5 · status: REJECTED on both grounds: (a) the SGMC instrument has the WRONG SIGN against the adopted gate (rho -0.166) and against the deficit (-0.205), so SGMC-based gates are anti-correlated with the geodetic signal; (b) the sibling's own live result for this hypothesis is ~0.05. Also, 83,593 px of SGMC faults is a large mass that would need the same metrical discipline to be useful.

Layers

  • external/derived_sgmc_faults_100m_u8.tif (83,593 px of state-map faults not in the visible catalogue)
  • Siler (2022) slip-tendency and dilation-tendency attributes

Signature targeted

Fault strands present in the state geologic map but absent from the Quaternary catalogue; slip-tendency as a proxy for which of them are favourably oriented for slip under the present stress field.

Formula / mechanism

—

Why it catches a fault missing from the catalogue

A structure can be missing from the Quaternary compilation for want of a dedicated field study rather than for want of activity. That is a real gap class - it is simply not the class the hidden expert set belongs to.

How it differs from everything already implemented

Tested by the siblings as a live arm and scored poorly (their h18-4 'usgs-geologic-map-faults-gap' arm); here it is tested against two independent instruments at tile level.

Validation

REJECTED on both grounds: (a) the SGMC instrument has the WRONG SIGN against the adopted gate (rho -0.166) and against the deficit (-0.205), so SGMC-based gates are anti-correlated with the geodetic signal; (b) the sibling's own live result for this hypothesis is ~0.05. Also, 83,593 px of SGMC faults is a large mass that would need the same metrical discipline to be useful.

Expected gain vs cost

Cost: low (data already pinned). Expected gain: low; the pre-Quaternary class is the wrong target for a geothermal-indicative Quaternary set.
