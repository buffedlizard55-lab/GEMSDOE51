H-EBasin-edge geophysics: buried range-front faultscomp_depth_to_base_surf (depth to basement surface), comp_cond_surf (electrical conductivity surface), comp_iso_grav_anom (isostatic gravity anomaly)Co-location of three independent expressions of one buried structure: (a) a step in depth-to-basement, (b) a resistivity contrast between bedrock and basin fill, (c) a gravity gradient. Implemented as normalised-convolution gradient magnitude, Hessian crestness and Hessian anisotropy of each field at 150 m and 400 m, plus a 9x9 coherence of the basement-step direction.A Great Basin range front buried under basin fill has almost no topographic expression, so a scarp-based catalogue systematically misses it, while its basin geometry is obvious in MT/airborne-EM depth-to-basement and in gravity. Range-front faults are also the geothermal targets the prize cares about. Individually depth_to_base_surf (AUC 0.512) and cond_surf (0.483) are near-uninformative; the information is in their co-location with a gravity gradient, which no shipped layer expresses.The existing stack uses these three fields only as raw amplitudes.
