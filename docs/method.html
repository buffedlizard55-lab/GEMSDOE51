Current method and decision — H53-A (2026-10-08)

H53-A is a rejected research candidate, not a submission. The task is prediction of fault geometry absent from a supplied known-fault catalogue, not geothermal-vent labels. Its new Stage 1 was evaluated as a coarse prior; Stage 2 fine placement was scored separately. Both stages use visible known-fault proxy truth, so neither result validates hidden expert labels.

Stage 1 — coarse geodetic strain-budget residual

The source equation is Kreemer et al. (2000), Eq. 3, for strain-rate tensor contribution from fault slip:

eps_dot_ij = (1/2) sum_k [L_k * u_dot_k / (A * sin(delta_k))]
                       * (n_i^k * m_j^k + n_j^k * m_i^k)

L is clipped trace-segment length, u_dot the selected rate component, A tile area, delta dip, n the unit fault normal and m the unit slip direction. The source and derivation are linked at Kreemer et al. (2000). With the project convention of summing actual clipped segments by 10 km tile, rates are converted from mm/year before summation. The tensor is contracted to the source-defined model dilatation and shear scalars, then compared componentwise with corresponding observed scalar tile means. It is **not** full observed-tensor minus full modeled-tensor subtraction and it does not locate a fine-scale fault.

Official shapefile audit. The field definition states SLIPRT2023 is the assigned rate in mm/year and SLIPRTNUM its numeric portion; all 1,126 clipped regional records and 84,331 projected segment pieces were matched to the preserved official DBF. The source does not say whether that rate is vertical displacement or total fault-plane slip. DIPDIRECT gives a cardinal direction, not a numeric dip angle; 60° normal and 90° strike-slip dips are assumptions. The primary SLIPSENSE interpretation is used, blank/unsupported senses are omitted, and SECONDARY is not added as a second full rate because no partition is documented. The README's listed sense codes omit observed RL/LL values in the clipped rows. The source writes 10E-9/yr; the primary case reads that arithmetically as 1e-8/yr while preserving 1e-9/yr and vertical-rate alternatives. See the full field/record audit and four-scenario Stage-1 receipt.

The registered primary residuals are R_dil = observed_dilatation - modeled_fault_dilatation and R_shear = observed_shear - modeled_fault_shear. The combined broad prior is the equal-weight mean of the empirical ranks of their positive parts, thresholded at the frozen q10. A positive residual remains ambiguous: off-fault strain, aseismic deformation, uncertain slip-rate component, dip/rake or trace geometry, codebook gaps, geodetic processing, and model error are all plausible explanations.

Stage 2 — fine-scale placement, evaluated independently

The tested detector is a fixed 50:50 probability blend of freshly fitted H-D and H-H arms, followed by strike-oriented STE (ridge scales 1/2/3.5 px, L9 line accumulator, continuity exponent 0.35, greedy spacing 2.4 px). Stage 1 only limits legal emission tiles; it is not a Stage-2 model feature or soft ranking weight. Six spatial blocks, 12 px boundary buffer, 250,000 seeded negatives, 250 HGB iterations, matched mass ratio 3.47, and 2 px known-catalogue exclusion were fixed by the preregistration.

Does Stage 1 dominate the full-map support?

The diagnostic has 44,069 points, all within the q10-approved mask. The full-map mask covers 90.1118% of the footprint, so point-share/area-share lift is 1.10973: by this global criterion, Stage 1 does not dominate where the points are placed. This does not mean the points are independent of the mask; one spatial holdout block approved only 54.98% of area. In addition, Stage 1's own catalogue-recall/area lift was approximately one, so the gate did not show useful enrichment.

Final-byte and uniqueness status

The new TIFF is one float32 band, EPSG:32611, exact 3730 × 3292 template grid/transform, finite values in [0,1], zero outside the footprint, and the one-file ZIP passes integrity checks. The local available-artifact gate passes. The broader pinned 54-repository family audit fetched and SHA-verified 365 unique Git blobs and found no byte duplicate, but support containment reached 1.000 (threshold 0.600); therefore the public-family uniqueness gate fails. It is not cleared as genuinely unique. Local format validity is not portal acceptance. Full receipts: artifact, public uniqueness, Stage 1, Stage 2.
