**Status at freeze:** written after reading `README.md`, `AGENTS.md`, the 2026-10-07 candidate ledgers and current evidence, and before implementing or scoring any 2026-10-08 candidate feature. No new competition slot has been used. Candidate ranks below are qualitative expected proxy-DTI benefit per implementation cost, not numeric forecasts. The only comparator reference is the repository's previous best *local visible-catalogue proxy* (six-fold mean 0.285340602656319); it is not a leaderboard score.

## Decision context and evidence boundary

- The organizer says the target is fault geometry missing from the existing USGS database, not direct geothermal-vent presence. The local spatial holdout uses visible catalogue faults as proxy truth and cannot validate genuinely undiscovered fault styles.
- The official scoring rule is distance-weighted Tversky, with alpha=0.2, beta=0.8 and a 300 m triangular distance kernel. At fixed prediction mass, a more selective/precise set may improve the score, but a particular artifact's outcome is not identifiable from its name or reported score alone.
- The highest available measured GEMSDOE51 experiment is the H-H/H-D 50:50 blend plus fixed STE emission at 0.285340602656319 mean DTI on six spatial folds. Its truth is the visible catalogue; it is **not** the organizer score. A previous same-environment reconstruction is already recorded at 0.28531515486970865 (−0.0000254478; outside the earlier 1e-5 bit-harness tolerance), with folds 1–5 exact and a small fold-0 emission tie difference. This discrepancy remains explicit. **Before implementing/scoring K2**, I amend the new-candidate promotion screen to require the candidate both (a) exceed the frozen historical best 0.285340602656319 and (b) beat the fresh, same-fold, same-environment gated H-D/H-H baseline with positive paired mean delta and at least 4/6 folds positive. The old bit-reproduction result is reported as a harness irregularity, not silently relaxed or hidden; it is not a veto after this measured discrepancy is already documented, because the dual comparator is stricter on the candidate score itself. No candidate result is available at this amendment.
- The supplied score “0.2778 for H33-2-B2” and the user's “0.3195 current high” are not organizer-authenticated records in this repository. GEMSDOE32's owner page describes its own 0.2747 number as projected and labels H33-2-B2 UNSCORED. No mechanism will be inferred from either number.
- GDR's official QFault v2 field definitions verify `SLIPRT2023` in mm/year and `SLIPRTNUM` as its numeric part. The slip-component convention and per-fault dip/rake remain uncertain. The geodetic README writes units as `10E-9/yr`, which is ambiguous between 1e-9 and 1e-8 in this use. These will be sensitivity scenarios, not post-hoc chosen facts.

## Frozen K2 experiment (no tuning after results)

### K2 feature operator frozen before its implementation

- Use raw, valid `depth_to_base_surf` and `cond_surf` samples on the provided grid. At Gaussian derivative scales `sigma=2 px` and `sigma=5 px`, estimate the basement-edge normal from the normalized-convolution basement gradient. At each scale, retain only centers at or above the 75th percentile of basement-gradient magnitude (threshold estimated without labels); write zero K2 signal elsewhere in the valid footprint.
- With normal `n` and tangent `t`, sample conductivity along five tangent offsets `[-5, -2.5, 0, 2.5, 5] px`. For each offset `j`, compute `d_j = C(x+j*t) - 0.5*(C(x+j*t+3*sigma/2*n) + C(x+j*t-3*sigma/2*n))`. Reject the center if any of the 15 required conductivity samples is invalid/outside support. For each sigma, output two channels: (a) the average-tie empirical rank of `median_j(abs(d_j))` among retained basement-edge centers, set to zero off-edge; (b) `abs(mean_j(d_j))/(mean_j(abs(d_j))+1e-12)`, in `[0,1]`, as signed-contrast persistence, also zero off-edge. Both contrast signs are accepted; this is not a “conductive means fault” rule. The two scale-specific outputs are named `k2_cond_cross_rank_s20`, `k2_cond_sign_persist_s20`, `k2_cond_cross_rank_s50`, and `k2_cond_sign_persist_s50`.
- Use mask-aware bilinear samples and normalized Gaussian derivatives with a halo sufficient for the largest filter and offset. These four static layers are computed without labels; no geometry from any prior prediction raster enters the model. No parameter sweep is allowed after the spatial holdout.

### Stage 1 — broad physical residual prior, separately scored

1. Use the GDR/USGS INGENIOUS QFault v2 attributes and the checked 84,331 projected trace segments; use only trace records with a numeric rate and recognized sense. Convert mm/year to m/year. Retain the official field-definition match receipt and report omitted/unsupported records.
2. For each 20 km × 20 km tile, sum clipped trace-segment tensors using Kreemer et al. (2000), Eq. 3:
   \[
   \dot\epsilon_{ij,t}=\frac{1}{2}\sum_k \frac{L_{k,t}\dot u_k}{A_t\sin\delta_k}\,m_{ij}^{(k)},\qquad
   m_{ij}^{(k)}=n_i m_j+n_j m_i.
   \]
   Here `L` is clipped trace length (m), `u` is slip rate (m/year), `A` is tile area (m²), and the horizontal unit moment tensor is built from local trace strike, dip and slip-direction assumptions. `mu` cancels from the moment-rate/volume expression. Normal traces use an explicit 60° fallback dip; strike-slip traces use 90°; the normal rate is evaluated under both vertical-component and fault-plane-component interpretations. Unsupported slip sense is omitted and counted, not silently assigned.
3. From the summed horizontal tensor derive dilation and the source-defined scalar shear (minimum absolute principal strain when principal strains have opposite signs, otherwise zero). For each documented component/unit scenario, subtract predicted tile dilation from observed geodetic dilation and predicted tile shear from observed geodetic shear. Because the official README's `10E-9/yr` text and the source slip component are ambiguous, preserve both 1e-9 and 1e-8 rate scalings and both slip-component assumptions. Rank each signed scalar residual across eligible tiles, average dilation- and shear-residual ranks per scenario, then average scenarios. This rank ensemble is only a broad-tile prior, **not** a calibrated absolute strain field or evidence that every positive residual is a missing fault.
4. Use 200-pixel (20 km) tiles to avoid claiming 10 km spatial resolution from a geodetic layer whose source documentation describes ~15 km characteristic resolving scale and ~0.2°×0.15° model cells. Approve tiles at or above the 10th percentile of the residual-prior score (q10; approximately 90% of eligible area). The fine model may emit **only** inside approved tiles.
5. Stage-1 validation: five deterministic whole-trace splits (seed 51). Remove all held-out trace records from the slip-budget term, then report approved-area share, held-out trace-pixel recall/lift and repeated uniform-in-approved versus uniform-in-footprint DTI. This is a scattered trace holdout (not a spatially independent lockbox); report the limitation. Stage 1 never places points.

### Stage 2 — fine-scale K2 model, separately scored

- Six fixed contiguous 3×3 spatial blocks from `gems51.holdout.make_folds`, 12-pixel buffer; train the existing HistGradientBoosting pipeline on training-visible catalogue positives plus 250,000 sampled background pixels. Train fold-matched H-D, H-H and H-H+K2 feature arms with the same seed plan and estimator settings. The candidate probability field is the fixed 50:50 blend of H-D and H-H+K2; the baseline is the frozen 50:50 H-D/H-H blend.
- Use fixed mass `round(3.47 × fold truth pixels)`, fixed STE L9 / w=0.35 / spacing=2.4 emission, fixed 200 m known-catalogue exclusion, and the fold-specific q10 Stage-1 mask. No H33 TIFF or earlier TIFF is used as model input. Run the current baseline reproduction before candidate scoring; if it misses 0.285340602656319 by more than 1e-5, report a harness failure and do not promote.
- **Promotion is fail-closed:** candidate gated mean must exceed the frozen 0.2853406 local best and the same-fold gated baseline; paired mean delta must be positive with at least 4/6 folds positive; Stage-1 approved area must be >=2/3; every emitted point must be in an approved tile; a same-mask uniform control must be beaten by >=0.002 DTI. All numbers remain visible-catalogue proxies, not organizer scores. No competition slot is used unless every validation, provenance, local format and scoped uniqueness gate passes.
- A diagnostic TIFF may be created after a failed experiment only if it is visibly marked **RESEARCH ONLY — DO NOT SUBMIT**. Do not describe it as portal accepted. Record the filename, unique identifier, hash, optional note, local `[0,1]`/CRS/transform checks, support-uniqueness scope, and stage-one dominance diagnostics.
