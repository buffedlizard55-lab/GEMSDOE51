## Stage 1 — physical fault-accommodation residual as a coarse tile prior

This experiment kept Stage 1 separate from fine-scale prediction. It used the source-matched INGENIOUS/QFault v2 slip-rate and sense attributes and projected source trace geometry. The rate field definition verifies `SLIPRT2023` in mm/year and `SLIPRTNUM` as its numeric portion; the local 1,126-row mirror was matched against the checked source attributes. Trace-segment tensors were summed in 20 km tiles using Kreemer et al. (2000), Eq. 3. The summed tensor supplies fault accommodation; its dilation and source-defined scalar shear were subtracted from the supplied **dilatation and shear** geodetic layers under declared scenarios:

- residual dilation = scaled observed dilation − fault-tensor dilation;
- residual shear = scaled observed shear − fault-tensor scalar shear;
- geodetic unit scales: `1e-9/yr` and `1e-8/yr`;
- slip-rate component scenarios: total fault-plane and vertical component, with an explicit 60° normal-fault dip assumption;
- residual ranks are averaged within each scenario and across the four scenarios; the resulting q10 mask approves broad tiles only.

This is physical scalar subtraction before ranking, **not** the older rank-space `observed second invariant − fault second invariant` diagnostic. The prior is not an absolute-strain inversion and is not used as a fine-scale point placer. Dip/rake simplifications and the geodetic README's ambiguous `10E-9/yr` notation remain limitations.

## Format, serialized-range and confinement checks

The final serialized GeoTIFF is one-band `float32`, EPSG:32611, 3730 × 3292, with transform `[100, 0, 243350, 0, -100, 4508550]`, matching the competition template. Every in-footprint value is finite and in `[0,1]` (min 0, max 1); all 7,111,787 outside-footprint cells are NaN/NoData; 44,069 in-footprint cells equal 1. The script re-reads the written bytes and fails on non-finite or out-of-range in-footprint values, a positive outside the approved q10 domain, an outside cell that is not NaN, or a changed point count. The local verifier reports `format_valid=true` and `portal_legal=true`; `all_cells_finite_in_range=false` is expected because the required outside-footprint nulls are NaN.

All 44,069 points are inside the full-map q10 approved region. The full-map gate covers 90.3536% of the footprint, so point occupancy is only 1.1068 times the area share. This is a broad gate and weak concentration, not a Stage-1 win. The range guard addresses the known error text “Predicted values must be in range [0, 1]” for serialized values inside the footprint; the prior triggering file is unknown, and no local check proves portal acceptance. The TIFF tags and receipt say `SAFE_TO_SUBMIT=NO` and `PORTAL_VALIDATION=NOT_PERFORMED`.
