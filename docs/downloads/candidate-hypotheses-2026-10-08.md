# Candidate slate and frozen test plan — 2026-10-08

**This four-candidate slate was preregistered before implementing or scoring the componentwise budget-q10 H53-A operationalization.** Target: improve discovery of fault geometry absent from the supplied known-fault catalogue. It is not a geothermal-vent classifier. Expected directions below are qualitative research judgments, not predicted scores. Novelty claims are limited to this repository, its archived hypothesis ledger, and the bounded 54-repository public-artifact/document inventory; they are not global literature claims. The budget-q10 H53-A variant has since been tested and not promoted; it is distinct from the separate finite-lag H53-A experiment, whose Stage 2 was blocked before candidate scoring. See the dated outcome section at the end and the finite-lag receipts in `README.md`.

## H53-A frozen protocol

### Stage 1 — coarse prior, evaluated alone

1. Use the 10 km vector tensor grid, exact official source trace geometries clipped by tile, and the source-documented geodetic scalar definitions. The equation is Kreemer et al. (2000), Eq. 3:
   `eps_dot_ij = (1/2) sum_k [L_k * u_dot_k / (A * sin(delta_k))] * (n_i*m_j + n_j*m_i)`.
2. Primary physical scenario: source rate interpreted as total fault-plane rate, normal dip 60° (assumption), strike-slip dip 90° (assumption), use primary `SLIPSENSE` only, omit unknown/unsupported senses, and read `10E-9/yr` arithmetically as `1e-8/yr`. No `SECONDARY` rate is added: the source gives no separate rate partition, so adding the full numeric rate twice is unjustified. Report sensitivity to vertical-rate interpretation and `1e-9/yr` notation as separate, non-promotable scenarios.
3. Compute signed dimensional residuals `R_dil = observed_dilatation - modeled_fault_dilatation` and `R_shear = observed_shear - modeled_fault_shear` in the same source-unit scenario. The preregistered combined prior is the equal-weight mean of empirical ranks of `max(R_dil, 0)` and `max(R_shear, 0)` across valid 10 km tiles. Preserve and report each raw residual; do not call the combined score a tensor residual.
4. Use the same 3×3 spatial blocks and six truth-bearing folds as the Stage-2 protocol. Before calculating the modeled-fault tensor for a fold, exclude every source trace record whose geometry intersects that held block plus the 12-pixel training buffer. Use only held-block catalogue pixels as Stage-1 proxy truth. Freeze the broad `q10` threshold (approve score >= the 10th percentile, nominally about 90% of footprint); do not choose another threshold after seeing results.
5. Report Stage-1 area, held-out truth recall, lift (recall / area share), tile-rank association, a geodetic-only equal-weight mean of positive dilation/shear ranks without fault subtraction, and sensitivity cases separately. This is a visible-catalogue proxy, not hidden-label validation. Treat the residual as potentially off-fault, aseismic, a rate/dip/rake error, or geodetic/model error.

### Stage 2 — fine-scale placement, evaluated separately

- Fit the fixed H-D and H-H models fold-by-fold on the existing 58 base features plus their already defined extras; use the fixed 50:50 probability blend. Stage 1 is **only** a coarse allowed-domain mask (zero soft-score weight), not a Stage-2 feature or pixel placer.
- Holdout: six 3×3 spatial blocks, 12-pixel training buffer, 250,000 negative samples (seed `1000 + fold`), HGB 250 iterations, fixed STE ridge scales 1/2/3.5 px / L9 / continuity exponent 0.35 / spacing 2.4 px, mass ratio 3.47, and 2-pixel exclusion around known catalogue geometry. Refit the fold-specific H-D/H-H Stage-2 arms. Compute STE scores over the ungated fold-valid domain, then apply q10 only as the allowed-emission mask; this keeps the physical Stage-1 score out of Stage-2 ranking. Report fresh ungated reproduction and q10-constrained results separately.
- **Stage-2 clarification amendment (2026-10-08, before Stage-2 scoring):** the initial draft's same-model q10 comparator was not an independent comparison. No result-dependent threshold, weight, or feature change is made. The q10-constrained H-D/H-H blend is compared against its fresh same-fold ungated counterpart and the frozen ungated best; a seeded same-domain uniform emitter is a separate control.
- Promotion requires all of: (a) fresh ungated baseline within `1e-5` of 0.285340602656319; (b) q10-constrained Stage-2 mean exceeds both that frozen ungated best and fresh ungated baseline; (c) paired q10-constrained mean delta over same-fold ungated baseline is positive and at least 4/6 folds improve; (d) every emitted Stage-2 point is inside approved tiles; (e) approved area is at least 2/3 and implied confinement lift is at most 1.5; and (f) q10-constrained Stage 2 beats a seeded, uniform spaced fill of the same allowed domain by at least 0.002 mean proxy DTI. These rules are not relaxed after results.
- Full-map diagnostic output, if generated, uses a fixed 44,069-point budget solely to match the existing local-best artifact's mass; it is not an estimate of hidden truth size. A TIFF may be generated after the holdout only as a clearly marked research artifact if promotion fails. That is **not** permission to spend a competition slot. No portal acceptance or hidden-set score can be established locally.

## Outcome — separate componentwise budget-q10 variant, after preregistered evaluation

**The componentwise budget-q10 H53-A variant in this slate was not promoted. No competition slot is authorized.** The slate above was saved before its residual-mask build and holdout; the outcomes below reference separate, preserved receipts. This is not the finite-lag H53-A experiment; that candidate's Stage 2 was blocked on baseline reproduction and has no candidate score or TIFF.

### Stage 1 — coarse holdout

Primary scenario: source `SLIPRT2023`/`SLIPRTNUM` assigned rate converted from mm/year using the registered `1e-8` geodetic factor, total fault-plane rate interpretation, primary `SLIPSENSE` only, 60° normal and 90° strike-slip dips as assumptions, and 10 km source-geometry tensor tiles. The tensor formula is Kreemer et al. (2000), Eq. 3, linked above. Official field definitions confirm **mm/year**, but do not resolve whether the number is vertical or total fault-plane motion; `DIPDIRECT` is cardinal dip direction, not dip angle. The numeric source code documentation omits observed `RL`/`LL` codes in the local clipped data, another documented irregularity. See [`evidence/official_attribute_audit_20261008.json`](../evidence/official_attribute_audit_20261008.json).

Across the six registered blocks, the primary residual q10 mask approved **91.6386%** of held-out area, recalled **91.6491%** of visible-catalogue truth, and had recall/area lift **0.999618** with tile-rank Spearman **0.060803**. The geodetic-only control had lift **0.996075**. This is no meaningful demonstrated residual enrichment. Alternative unit/rate-component scenarios are fully preserved in [`evidence/h53_stage1_holdout_20261008.json`](../evidence/h53_stage1_holdout_20261008.json). A positive residual is not proof or a location of an unmapped fault: it may be off-fault or aseismic deformation, incorrect rate/component/dip/rake or geometry assumptions, geodetic smoothing, or other data/model error.

### Stage 2 — fine placement, separate six-fold test

The fixed Stage-2 H-D/H-H 50:50 blend was freshly reconstructed and evaluated separately. Its ungated mean was **0.285315155**, or **−0.000025448** below the frozen **0.285340603** best, outside the registered `1e-5` reproducibility tolerance. The q10-constrained candidate mean was **0.266509778**, a **−0.018830824** difference versus that frozen best and **0/6 positive paired folds**. It beat same-domain uniform spaced emission by 0.051443, but this does not cure the loss to the frozen local best. The outcome is known-catalogue proxy DTI only, not hidden-label validation. Receipt and all fold values: [`evidence/h53_stage2_holdout_20261008.json`](../evidence/h53_stage2_holdout_20261008.json).

The registered *mean* non-dominance diagnostics passed (mean holdout mask area 0.9164, mean confinement lift 1.0912), but one block was an important instability: mask area **0.5498**, implied confinement lift **1.8188**. Do not portray the broad mask as uniformly permissive across spatial blocks.

### Full-map research TIFF and final-byte gates

A new 44,069-point full-map diagnostic was emitted only after holdout. It is a fresh prediction build, not a copied prediction raster. Local byte checks pass (one float32 band, EPSG:32611, exact required grid/transform, finite `[0,1]` values, zeros outside); its one-file ZIP also passes. On the full map the mask covers **90.1118%** of the footprint and contains **100%** of emitted points, so point-share/area-share lift is **1.10973**; by that global diagnostic the support is not chiefly concentrated by the Stage-1 mask, although it inherits the mask and the holdout-fold exception above remains. Neither fact is a submission clearance.

The local artifact uniqueness gate passed, but the broader pinned 54-repository family gate **failed**: all 365 unique TIFF Git blobs were fetched and SHA-verified, maximum support Jaccard was 0.184778, and maximum containment was **1.0**, above the registered 0.6 ceiling. Zero byte duplicates do not negate support containment. Explicit status: **RESEARCH ONLY — DO NOT SUBMIT; not cleared as genuinely unique for the public family**. Receipts: [`evidence/h53_artifact_20261008T030347Z.json`](../evidence/h53_artifact_20261008T030347Z.json), [`evidence/h53_public_uniqueness_20261008.json`](../evidence/h53_public_uniqueness_20261008.json), and the final-byte checks beside the TIFF.

**Final status for this alternative slate:** the budget-q10 H53-A variant was **NOT PROMOTED** on Stage 2 and broad support uniqueness **FAIL**. Do not relax the frozen tolerance or uniqueness threshold. This TIFF is a research diagnostic only; it is not cleared for download as a submission or for a slot. No portal attempt, portal acceptance, competition upload, organizer score, hidden-set result, or global uniqueness claim is made.
